## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: finished landscape16:9 promotional illustration for Midnight Studios Just Jump!, used in the Games collection and editable Journal cards. The supplied images are ACTUAL GAMEPLAY references. Make a new polished landscape composition faithfully inspired by their game world and character proportions, not a screenshot crop.
Essential gameplay: this is a VERTICAL endless jumping game. Show a clearly UPRIGHT orange floppy-eared puppy rising straight UP through a column of small floating platforms at successive heights. Head above torso, feet together below body, front paws slightly raised, subtle narrow vertical boost/light trail directly below. NO sideways running pose, broad horizontal leap, or cliff-to-cliff jump.
Essential character correction: use the slender in-game puppy silhouette from the reference. Lean, lightly built torso and waist, small cream chest/belly patch, reasonably long slim legs, normal compact paws, expressive black eyes and black nose, short floppy orange ears and a small blue backpack/boost pack matching the game. Body is visibly narrower than head. Slim torso, no round pot belly, no squat infant body, no inflated cheeks, no obese/chubby plush toy. Keep it a charming healthy stylized game character, not skinny bones or realistic dog anatomy. The full figure occupies about one quarter of image height and remains entirely inside the composition.
Essential platform correction: SMALL thin stepping platforms, rectangular top surfaces with gently beveled edges, lime-green tops and ochre/peach sides, matching actual game. Each platform roughly11–16% of total image width, shallow thickness, NOT giant cubes, chunky boulders or huge cliff platforms. Place one below the puppy at lower middle, one above and slightly to its right at upper middle, and several at staggered heights further down/up. Strong vertical spacing and coherent upward route, modest horizontal offsets.
Cyan sky, soft white clouds, distant blue cliff silhouettes, scattered geometric evergreen trees on distant edges. Bright warm sunlight, crisp tactile stylized3D materials, sophisticated game key art lighting. Foreground stays open; use atmospheric depth rather than giant cropped blocks. Vivid cyan/ochre/lime/orange palette matching the references and other premium game covers. No text, logos, letters, fake HUD, buttons, counters, UI panels, frames or watermark. This is promotional concept art, not claimed to be actual gameplay.
